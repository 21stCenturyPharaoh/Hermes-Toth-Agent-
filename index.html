GENESIS 1:28 — STEWARDSHIP

CREATE • RECORD • STEWARD • ACCOUNT • DELIVER

Genesis 1:28 is used here as a stewardship framework: human beings are responsible for what they create, cultivate, organize and place into the world.

Metatron therefore does not merely move information. The system records the movement of information and connects it to people, organizations, missions, commerce and documentation.

THE METATRON SCRIBE PROTOCOL
