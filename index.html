A practical covenant

People before platform

Clear disclosures. Voluntary participation. Consent-based communications. Transparent fees where applicable. Respect for local law and each participant’s right to review terms before proceeding.

The platform is designed to support human decisions—not replace them with automated claims or fictional authority.

The actual business model

More than a showcase of nine businesses

The Choice Businesses are early examples and visible case studies—not the entire business model. The platform is intended to serve multiple participant roles and repeatable workflows.

Business / organization onboarding

Capture the participant’s details, services, preferred contact route, consent, and relevant documentation. Human review and applicable legal requirements remain in force.

Registry visibility

Present participating organizations with clear descriptions, contact pathways, and records. Listing is not a guarantee of quality, sales, licensing, or revenue.

Affiliate and referral pathway

Where enabled, affiliates can introduce interested customers to participating businesses or programs under disclosed terms. Any commissions or fees must be stated before participation.

Customer → provider connection

Customers review the offer, contact the provider, ask questions, and decide whether to transact. The provider remains responsible for its products, services, fulfillment, and customer obligations.

Program owner / service delivery

Program owners define their own scope, eligibility, pricing, fulfillment, and support. The Registry can organize the pathway without representing every program as the same legal or financial arrangement.

Records, follow-up, and stewardship

Consent-based records and follow-up help participants track requests, referrals, delivery status, and outcomes—with privacy-conscious handling and human correction routes.

Sun Tzu nod · Know the terrain

Strategy begins with understanding the actual conditions: local needs, customs requirements, reliable counterparties, delivery costs, and the people who will use the goods or services. The “terrain” is the real market—not a slogan.

Sun Tzu nod · Prepare before movement

Build trust through due diligence, written terms, transparent pricing, verified shipping and payment arrangements, and clear responsibility for customs, warranties, and delivery. Move only when the route is understood.

Local value first

Prioritize Liberian participation: distributors, repair services, translators, transport providers, digital and document support, retailers, and workforce development. Trade should create useful capacity on both sides.

Start with a pilot

Identify one practical product or service category, validate demand, confirm compliance and landed costs, and document outcomes before expanding. No guaranteed profits, jobs, or shipment volumes are implied.

“Win without fighting” is often associated with Sun Tzu’s strategic tradition; here it is used only as a metaphor for solving coordination problems through preparation, clarity, and mutually beneficial exchange—not as a political or military claim.
